## Conclusion

> The no-Muhtasib baseline still passed scenarios, proving the rest of the pipeline is robust. But it eliminated independent safety review: first-draft plans went directly to the human gate, shifting safety burden back to the operator. Muhtasib adds measurable governance quality at a measurable token cost.
